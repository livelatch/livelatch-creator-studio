## What is LCS?

LCS provides overlay assets and real-time stream tools that plug directly into the Livelatch ecosystem — giving creators richer, more interactive livestreams without leaving their broadcast setup.

Everything runs on your own machine. Your audience data, gift history, chat transcripts and sound library stay in Creator Studio's own app data folder.

## Features

### Live & Dashboard

- **Live Stream View** — real-time gifts, likes, follows, shares and concurrent viewer count
- **Rearrangeable panels** — drag every panel into the layout that suits you
- **Live chat** — pop it out into its own always-on-top window for a second monitor, with gift alerts, timestamps, zoom and a newest-on-top toggle
- **Chat word cloud** — what your chat is actually talking about over the last 5, 10, 30 or 60 minutes, with bot commands, mentions and filler filtered out
- **Exact follower count** — TikTok only publishes a rounded figure, so you can type the real one and have live gains stack on top of it

### Overlays

Every overlay is a transparent OBS browser source with its own copy-URL button.

- **End-of-Stream Credits** — a film-style rolling credits sequence built from everyone who supported the show, updating live
- **Credit Scene Customisation** — tailor the look and feel of your credits
- **Crowd Control panels** — a 😈 negative and 😇 positive panel showing viewers which gift buys which effect, with rows that light up when an effect fires, rotation for long lists, and full styling control. Built for Minecraft plus TikTok LIVE
- **Gift Videos** — plays the real TikTok gift animation when that gift lands: in the app, in a pop-out window for a second monitor, and as a transparent browser source. Clips queue and play one at a time, and any gift can be fired on demand for testing

### Sounds

- **A sound library inside the app** — find, keep, trim, organise and trigger sound effects without leaving Creator Studio. Everything you save is copied into the app's own folder, so moving the original file or updating the app changes nothing
- **Freesound search** — search the whole Freesound catalogue in-app, with name, length, file size and format shown before you commit, and a play button to audition
- **Bring your own** — upload MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC, M4A, AAC, AIFF and Opus files, several at a time
- **Waveform trimming** — drag handles for in and out points, arrow keys for the last tenth of a second, and a Reset that always gets the original back. Your audio is never written over
- **Favourites and #hashtag categories** — star what you reach for and tag anything, with autocomplete so you don't end up with three spellings of the same tag
- **Per-sound and master volume** — balance sounds against each other and set the overall stream level as two separate jobs
- **Sound triggers** — point a gift at one or more categories and it plays a random sound from them, so the same gift never sounds identical twice. Overlap limits, streak handling and a coin floor are all configurable
- **Plays in the app and on stream** — a dedicated OBS browser source sends gift sounds out to your viewers
- **Record your own voice** — a mic picker, a live input meter, and an effects rack with pitch, speed, reverb, flanger, echo, drive, tone, robot and tremolo, plus presets like Chipmunk, Deep, Demon, Robot, Radio, Cathedral, Underwater, Spacey and Stutter

### Audience & AI

- **Viewer Hub** — everyone who has engaged with your streams, kept separately per creator, with lifetime totals and a gift-by-gift breakdown for anyone who has gifted
- **Full gift history** — every gift is recorded with its name, size and time, so you can ask which gift, from whom, and when
- **Chat transcripts** — saved per creator and per day, with gifts, follows, shares and subscribes interleaved so the conversation reads in context
- **Ask AI (MCP)** — connect Claude, Cursor, Codex, VS Code or any MCP-speaking app with one config block, then ask things like *"who were my top 10 gifters this month?"* or *"what times of night earn the most?"*. It runs entirely on your own computer and uploads nothing
- **Generate report** — a wizard that builds a ready-to-paste prompt for a stream report, with timeframe, topics and length of your choosing
- **Archive a creator** — move one creator's totals, gift log, transcripts and sentiment scores out of the way to start counting fresh. Nothing is deleted, and archives can be restored

### Account

- **Sign in with LatchID** — Google or an emailed sign-in code, kept signed in between restarts and encrypted with Windows' own protection
- **My LatchID tab** — your profile, connected accounts, email preferences and passkeys
- **Livelatch Pro** — on Pro there is no API key to find: Creator Studio connects on Livelatch's own TikTool key, with **Use mine** there if you would rather keep your own. Everyone else gets the key field and a link to grab a free one
